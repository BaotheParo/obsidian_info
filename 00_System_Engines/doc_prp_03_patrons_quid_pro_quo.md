Patrons Aren’t Allies
While both patrons and allies are NPCs the players might think of as
friendly, they are not the same thing. A patron is a character that might
not share the party’s goals but may decide to help them pursue their
goals anyway, enabling the PCs to work harder, better or faster. This
aid isn’t free: in exchange, the patron expects the party to advance the
patron’s own plans, knowingly or not.
