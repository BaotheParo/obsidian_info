Faction Tracking Made Simple
I track this information and keep these notes for one simple reason:
consistency. Proactive fantasy games are about the collision of PC
goals with the rest of the world. If I forget that the PCs thwarted a
certain necromancer’s evil ritual, I won’t be able to create faction
goals that react to the PC goals in a satisfying way. Keeping track of
factions, their goals and accomplishments helps keep me honest.
